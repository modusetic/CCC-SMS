Privacy Policy & SMS Consent

Last updated: May 8, 2026

1. About This Service

CCC SMS Scheduling is an appointment scheduling service that uses SMS (text messaging) to coordinate meeting times between an organizer and their contacts. Messages are sent on behalf of the organizer who initiated the scheduling request.

SMS Consent & Opt-In
By replying to a scheduling SMS sent by this service, you consent to receive follow-up SMS messages related to that scheduling request. Message frequency varies based on the number of exchanges needed to confirm a meeting time. Standard message and data rates may apply.

2. How Opt-In Works

You receive an initial SMS from this service because an organizer who has your contact information initiated a scheduling request with you. Your continued participation in the SMS conversation constitutes consent to receive further messages related to that specific scheduling thread.

No opt-in is required to view this policy. Only individuals who reply to a scheduling SMS are considered participants in the program.

3. How to Opt Out

You can opt out of receiving SMS messages from this service at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive a single confirmation message and no further scheduling messages will be sent to your number.

For help, reply HELP to any message.

4. Information We Collect

  • Your phone number (used solely to deliver and receive scheduling messages)
  • The content of SMS messages exchanged during a scheduling conversation
  • The meeting time confirmed during the conversation

Conversation data is retained for a maximum of 7 days and then automatically deleted.

5. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to:

  • Conduct the scheduling conversation on behalf of the organizer
  • Confirm and book the agreed meeting time
  • Notify the organizer of the confirmed appointment

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or phone number with any third party for marketing or advertising purposes.

Your phone number and message content are shared only with the following service providers as necessary to operate the scheduling service:

  • Twilio — SMS delivery
  • Google (Gemini API) — AI-assisted conversation processing
  • Upstash — temporary conversation state storage
  • Google Calendar — calendar event creation on behalf of the organizer

7. Data Retention

Scheduling conversation data is automatically deleted after 7 days regardless of whether a meeting time was confirmed. No long-term storage of your personal information is maintained.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or to request deletion of your data before the automatic 7-day expiry, reply STOP to any scheduling SMS or contact the organizer who initiated the scheduling request directly.
